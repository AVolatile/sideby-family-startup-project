# Sideby portfolio export guide

## Lead image: 1280 × 960 / 4:3

Use `portfolio-highlight.png`, generated by `scripts/export-assets.mjs`. Its composition combines the paired-path identity and positioning, a parent Today screen, a teen permissions screen, and a campaign message on paper, vermilion, and lilac. Keep the original 4:3 ratio. No laptop frame is needed.

For another composition, place the Sideby wordmark and **Room to grow. A plan to go.** in the upper left; overlap two mobile screens on the right; add a cropped **“I’m here.”** poster below the headline. Leave enough unoccupied paper surface for the composition to breathe. Keep product copy readable at the final presentation size.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Lead:** `portfolio-highlight.png` — identity + product + campaign.
2. **Marketing:** `screenshots/homepage-desktop.png` — full page; crop the top 960 px for the hero.
3. **Parent / teen product:** `parent-today-mobile.png` and `teen-today-mobile.png` — pair them with a short explanation of household context versus personal ownership.
4. **Coordination:** calendar, rides, and check-in mobile screenshots — show one practical handoff.
5. **Trust:** permission request and agreement; teen goals; independence milestones — show the supportive product philosophy.
6. **Brand:** `brand-desktop.png` plus vector identity, palette, and icons — show the decisions and rules.
7. **Campaign:** `campaign-desktop.png`, the poster wall, social square/story variants, and app discovery artwork.
8. **Lifecycle:** five standalone HTML emails and push voice section — show product-to-marketing continuity.
9. **Case study:** `case-study-desktop.png` — use individual section crops alongside concise written context.

Screenshots are in `portfolio-assets/screenshots/`; creative sources are in `public/brand/`, `public/campaign/`, and `public/emails/`. Each PNG creative preserves the source SVG's specified dimensions.

## Additional capture suggestions

- 390 × 844 for mobile Today and core workflows.
- 1440 × 960 for desktop hero, brand system, campaign wall, and split product layout.
- Capture real live product interactions for request/approval states; the vector App Store assets are campaign excerpts, not a native application.
- Use a fixed demo persona, the October 13, 2026 scenario, and freshly reset data for consistent product screenshots.
- Remove cursor hover and focus rings only for a static presentation capture, never from the implemented app.
- Keep the concept disclosure in the case study and relevant portfolio copy.
